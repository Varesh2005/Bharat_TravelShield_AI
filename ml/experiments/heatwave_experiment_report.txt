6. KEY EMPIRICAL FINDINGS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Feed-forward ANN/MLP achieves the highest overall performance on the test set:
   Accuracy: 0.8109, F1: 0.7456, ROC-AUC: 0.9253, PR-AUC: 0.8759.
2. Superiority over Persistence Baseline:
   Persistence Recall collapsed to 0.4381 (440 false negatives) following the
   2021 nationwide anomaly. ANN/MLP reduced false negatives to 251 (Recall: 0.6794),
   improving F1 from 0.5510 to 0.7456.
3. Comparison against Tabular Baselines:
   ANN/MLP outperforms Logistic Regression C=0.1 (F1: 0.6220, Rec: 0.5045)
   and Random Forest depth=12 (F1: 0.7224, Rec: 0.6564).

7. PROBABILITY CALIBRATION AUDIT & METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Brier Score Decomposition:
   ANN Test Brier Score is 0.1387. Brier score decomposes into Uncertainty + Reliability - Resolution.
   A low Brier score indicates strong discrimination/resolution, but does not prove probability calibration.
2. Calibration Diagnostic Metrics:
   Expected Calibration Error (ECE): 0.1119 (11.19%)
   Maximum Calibration Error (MCE) : 0.3746 (37.46%)
3. Underconfidence in Mid-Range Probabilities:
   Raw ANN predictions between 0.10 and 0.50 severely underestimate actual heatwave occurrence rates
   (observed positive rates in these bins range from 52.2% to 72.5%).
   Raw ANN probabilities must be treated as discriminatory risk scores rather than calibrated event probabilities.
4. Persistence Baseline Hard-Label Comparison Caveat:
   The Previous-Year Persistence baseline produces deterministic binary predictions {0, 1}.
   Its Brier score (0.2911) reflects its hard-label misclassification rate (1 - Accuracy = 0.2911).
   It cannot be directly compared to continuous probabilistic Brier scores without recognizing this hard-label distinction.
